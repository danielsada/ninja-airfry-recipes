Cook times for the **Ninja Foodi 2-Basket Air Fryer with DualZone Technology** (DZ201 series, 8 qt), from Ninja's quick start guide. Amounts are **per zone**. The first time column is for cooking in one zone with the other empty; the second is for both zones cooking at once, which takes longer. Use Smart Finish to have two different foods finish together, or Match Cook to copy zone 1's settings to zone 2. Times are guides, so check proteins with a thermometer.
